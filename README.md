Problem Statement
Community safety incidents and gender-based violence (GBV) remain serious challenges in South Africa. People affected by these incidents may struggle to find reliable support, report concerns, document incidents, and navigate the appropriate response channels. Fragmented services, limited awareness of available resources, and fears surrounding privacy, stigma, and retaliation can make seeking help even more difficult. There is a need for an accessible, privacy-conscious platform that brings community safety resources, incident documentation, and GBV support pathways together in one place.
Solution
SizaMe is a community safety and GBV support platform developed by Team CtrlAltDefeat to help individuals document concerns, explore available support services, and navigate appropriate response pathways. Built around the principles of privacy, safety, and user choice, the platform brings together two key pathways: Community Response and GBV Response.

Through features such as a personal incident diary, evidence organisation, trusted support resources, and accessible reporting pathways, SizaMe aims to simplify the process of finding help and managing incident-related information. The project also explores an optional GPS/GNSS tracking device concept to support location-based safety applications.

SizaMe's vision is to make community safety and GBV support more accessible, connected, and user-centred, empowering individuals to make informed decisions about their safety and the support they seek.
