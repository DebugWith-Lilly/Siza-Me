Connect Your Siza-Me Tracker

Follow these four simple steps to prepare and wear your Siza-Me tracker.

Step 1: Switch It On

Make sure your tracker is charged. Press its power button to switch it on.

Step 2: Enable Bluetooth

Open your phone's Settings and switch Bluetooth on.

Step 3: Attach the Sticker

Carefully peel off the sticker backing and attach the sticker to the tracker as shown. Make sure it is positioned correctly.

Step 4: Place It on Your Skin

Gently place the prepared tracker sticker onto clean, dry skin in the intended position. Follow the product's instructions and stop using it if irritation occurs.

Important Note

Bluetooth pairing alone does not provide live GPS tracking. Your tracker must support location sharing and be connected to a compatible tracking service integrated with Siza-Me.
